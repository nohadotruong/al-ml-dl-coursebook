## **Step 1: Define Goals**

   * Goal: “Build a model that predicts species with accuracy ≥ 0.9.”

## **Step 2: Data Collection & Exploration**

4. Load your dataset and check:

   * Data shape, types, missing values.
   * Histograms for numeric columns, bar charts for categorical.
   * Identify outliers and class imbalance.

5. Plot relationships:

   * Regression: correlation heatmap.
   * Classification: scatterplots colored by class.

6. Identify potential **data leakage**:

   * Columns that wouldn’t be available at prediction time.

---

## **Step 3: Data Preprocessing**

7. Handle missing values (mean, median, mode).
8. Encode categorical variables (One-Hot or Ordinal).
9. Scale numeric features where needed (StandardScaler).
10. Optional: feature engineering — e.g., combine columns or extract new info.

---

## **Step 4: Model Selection**

11. **Baseline practice:**

    * Regression → DummyRegressor (mean / median).
    * Classification → DummyClassifier (most_frequent / stratified).

12. **Basic models:**

    * Regression → LinearRegression, Ridge, Lasso.
    * Classification → LogisticRegression, KNN, DecisionTree.

13. **Advanced models (optional for beginners):**

    * RandomForest, GradientBoosting (XGBoost/LightGBM), SVM.

14. Pick **2–3 models**, train, and compare metrics on the same validation set.

---

## **Step 5: Training & Validation**

15. Split your dataset:

    * Random 70/15/15 or 80/20 for beginners.
    * Stratify for classification.

16. Optional: k-fold CV for stable evaluation.

17. Tune hyperparameters for at least one model (GridSearchCV or RandomizedSearchCV).

18. Wrap preprocessing + model in **Pipeline** to avoid leakage.

---

## **Step 6: Model Evaluation**

19. Evaluate metrics on validation / test set:

    * Regression → MAE, RMSE, R², residual plot.
    * Classification → Accuracy, F1, confusion matrix, ROC-AUC.

20. Compare **real model vs baseline**.

    * Ask: “Does my model actually learn something useful?”

21. Check for leakage risks:

    * Target, temporal, preprocessing.

---
